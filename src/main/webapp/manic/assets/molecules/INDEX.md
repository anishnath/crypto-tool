# Bundled molecules

Byte-exact **PubChem** records, fetched through the public PUG REST interface. Cite a compound by
its CID. `3-D` is the computed conformer (`molecule3`); `2-D` is the depiction record, laid out for
reading (`structure`) — see `../README.md` for why they are not interchangeable, and for the
licensing note.

**392 files, 192 molecules.** A blank in the 3-D
column means PubChem has no computed conformer for that compound — mostly salts, metal complexes
and the very large ones.
